Game Tutorial

Welcome to Web Mahjong! This is a game for four players.

The goal is to form a winning hand consisting of melds (sets of 3 or 4 identical tiles, or sequences of 3 tiles in the same suit) and a pair.

Controls: Double-click your tiles to discard. Use the buttons for actions like Chi, Pon, Kong, Hu when available.
